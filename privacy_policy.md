## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information
We collect the following personal information when you use our App:
- **Account Information:** Name, email address, and password when you create an account
- **Profile Preferences:** Selected state for DMV content, language preference
- **Subscription Data:** Subscription status, plan type, renewal and expiry dates, and the transaction identifier the App Store or Google Play gives us for your purchase. We do **not** receive or store your card number, billing address, or any payment credentials — Apple and Google process all payments and act as the merchant of record.

### 2.2 Usage Data
We automatically collect information about how you use our App:
- **Learning Progress:** Quiz scores, practice test results, study progress, saved questions
- **App Analytics:** Screen views, feature usage, session duration, app performance metrics
- **Device Information:** App version, platform type (iOS/Android), device model
- **Session Data:** Login times, session IDs for security purposes
- **Device Identifier (hashed):** To make sure the free trial can only be claimed once per device, we store a one-way hash of an identifier generated by the App on your device. It is not your device's serial number or advertising ID, it cannot be reversed to identify your device, and it is used for nothing except preventing repeated trials.
- **Crash Diagnostics:** If the App crashes or hits an unexpected error, we collect a diagnostic report containing the error and the sequence of code that produced it, your device model and operating system version, the App version, and whether the device was jailbroken or rooted. The report also carries your account identifier, your selected state, your language, and whether your subscription or trial was active at the time, so that we can reproduce the fault and, if you contact support, find the report for your account. These reports are used only to find and fix faults. They do not include your name, your email address, your quiz answers, your progress, your payment details, or the contents of any screen.

### 2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect
We do NOT collect:
- Location data or GPS information
- Contact lists or phone numbers
- SMS or call log data
- Health information
- Camera or photo access, microphone or audio recordings, or biometric information, unless you register as a driving instructor (see Section 2.4)
- Payment card numbers, bank details, or billing addresses
- Advertising identifiers (IDFA / AAID), and we do not track you across other apps or websites, unless you allow it (see Section 4.1)

### 2.4 Driving Instructors
This section applies only to people who register as driving instructors. Instructor registration is not open in the App yet. Students are never asked for camera or microphone access.

- **Camera and microphone:** The identity check uses them so that you can photograph your ID and record a short selfie video. We use them for nothing else.
- **Photos:** You can choose a profile photo, a licence photo or an ID document from your device.
- **Biometric data:** Our partner Didit compares your selfie video with the photo on your ID, which creates a map of your face geometry. The App asks for your written consent on a separate screen first, and that screen says how long the data is kept. Didit processes this data in the European Union.
- **What we receive:** Your name, date of birth, the result of the check and your ID’s expiry date. We do not receive the images or the face geometry, and we do not sell them.

### 4.1 Third-Party Service Providers
We share information with trusted third-party services that help us operate our App:

- Analytics and app performance monitoring

- Cloud Functions for app functionality
- File storage for images and content

**Payment Processors:**
- Google Play Store (for Android subscriptions)
- Apple App Store (for iOS subscriptions)
- Payment processing and subscription management


**Meta Platforms (Facebook and Instagram), only if you allow it:**
- We share your device's advertising identifier and events in the App (completing registration, starting a free trial, subscribing, and the product and price of a purchase), so we can measure which ads work and show our ads to people likely to be interested
- We do not share your name, email address or test results with Meta, which handles this data under its own Privacy Policy
- On iPhone you choose in the system prompt and can change it in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android you choose in the App and can change it in Profile → Ad measurement. If you do not allow it, your advertising identifier and these events are not shared, and the App works the same
- On Android, Meta's software in the App still connects to Meta when the App starts, to fetch its settings, so Meta can see your device's IP address and that it runs the App. This happens before you choose and after you say no

## 7. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act:

### 7.1 Right to Know
You can request information about the personal information we collect, use, and share about you.

### 7.2 Right to Delete
You can request deletion of personal information we have collected about you (subject to certain exceptions).

### 7.3 Right to Opt-Out
You have the right to opt-out of the sale of personal information. **Note: We do not sell personal information.** If you allow ad measurement, sending data to Meta may count as "sharing" for cross-contextual behavioral advertising under the CCPA. ### 7.4 Non-Discrimination
We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights.

To exercise these rights, contact us at driveusaservice@driveusallc.com.
