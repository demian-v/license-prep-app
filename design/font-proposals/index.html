Fonts: 5 proposals

Each font set in the real Тесты and Экзамен content, in Russian (the longest strings), with Ukrainian and Polish below and the timer digits. Every candidate was checked against all 193 characters used in the five locale files and has tabular figures; Jost (no Ukrainian Ї, є) and Nunito (no tabular figures) were ruled out. All are free under the SIL Open Font License.

Geometric-humanist, what ships today. Wide, open letterforms; bold weights get heavy in Cyrillic.

OnestSIL OFL · free

3 · Soft

RubikSIL OFL · free

Slightly rounded corners on every stroke: the typographic twin of Solar's rounded icons and the Bento cards. Friendly and approachable. Lacks the → arrow used in one error message (iOS would fill it from the system font).
