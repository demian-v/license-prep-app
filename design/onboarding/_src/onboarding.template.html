DriveUSA · first-run onboarding · chosen flow

A greeting, then the real screens

One Line-style greeting card, then six screenshot steps. Each step dims a real screenshot and lifts one element and its tab. Nothing opens the real app until «Начать», so the cache can load in the background. «Назад» steps back and «Пропустить» goes straight in. Tap a row in the script to jump to that step.

#ScreenHighlightTitle · text (ru)
Greeting motion
The car-and-bubble drawing draws itself in and the bubble floods blue over 0.8 s (60 ms stagger), then the car idles with spinning wheels on a moving road. «Начнём» is a wide pill. On the first step it shrinks into the round Next button, which then springs from dot to dot.
Step motion
The greeting card fades back as the screenshot rises in with a spring. Each highlight pops by 4 %, and the white ring springs to the next target. A new screen swaps the screenshot card sideways like a page (right when going forward, left when going back). «Начать» zooms the screenshot into the full screen.
