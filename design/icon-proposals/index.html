Icon sets: 5 proposals

Every icon the app draws, in each candidate set, placed in our own UI: the meniscus tab bar, the exam bar (amber timer, red saved heart), Bento tiles and answer verdicts. In the grid below each, outline is in ink and filled in blue. Every set has an outline/filled pair for all glyphs the app uses.
