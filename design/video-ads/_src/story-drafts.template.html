DriveUSA · first-run onboarding

Three ways to welcome a new driver

Each phone is a live prototype: tap the round button (or the highlighted ring) for the next step, and use Replay to start again. A and B tell the story (theory, DMV exam, instructor, driving), then walk through screenshots of the app with the tab bar highlighted. C skips the story and walks through the screens in more detail. None of them opens the real app until the last «Начать», so the app has time to load its cache in the background. Instructors is left out of the screenshots because it gets its own onboarding.

A

Line

The BERD reference, redrawn in our blue: drawings that draw themselves.

Motion
Each drawing draws in over 0.8 s, then whooshes out sideways with a blur. The round button springs to the next dot and becomes «Начать» on the last card. Then the story card becomes a screenshot of the app: the screenshot dims, one tab lifts onto a white square, and a ring springs from tab to tab. Changing screens swaps the screenshot card like a page. «Начать» zooms the screenshot into the real app.
To build in Flutter
CustomPainter with PathMetric for the drawing, and the same screenshot steps as C. No new packages.
B

Beep

Our mascot lives the whole story, from the book to the open road.

Motion
Beep stays on stage while the world slides past. Each Next is a hop, and the pose changes mid-air: reading, then the exam with a PASSED stamp, then the instructor car rolls in, then the open road with confetti. In the screenshot steps Beep peeks from behind the screenshot and looks at the highlighted tab.
To build in Flutter
Beep poses exported as SVG from the meme generator (flutter_svg is already in the app), moved with AnimationController. No new packages.
C

Real pages

No story and no drawings: screenshots of the app, one highlight at a time.

Motion
Kept calm. Each step lifts one element plus its tab out of the dimmed screenshot and gives it a small pop. Moving to another screen swaps the screenshot card, and the bead slides to the new tab like a real tap.
To build in Flutter
Screenshots are drawn from static widget copies (or PNGs) with highlight rectangles, so the real screens and their data aren't touched. Cheapest of the three. Screenshots need a refresh when the screens change.
